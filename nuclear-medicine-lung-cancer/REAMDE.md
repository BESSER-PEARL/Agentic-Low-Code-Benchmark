# Case Study: Nuclear Medicine for Lung Cancer

This case study is inspired by the use case described in the paper *"A Conceptual Model-Based Platform for Semi-Automatic Report Generation to Streamline Medical Diagnostics of Lung Cancer and PET/CT Data Management"*. PET/CT is a nuclear medicine diagnostic test that combines functional and anatomical imaging and is widely used in oncology to detect and stage tumors. After the examination, physicians must manage the resulting data and produce a structured report, motivating the need for dedicated information systems to support this process. The paper describes the relevant domain and its requirements for managing lung cancer PET/CT examinations.

This folder contains six variations of a web application for the case study, with increasing requirements complexity.
